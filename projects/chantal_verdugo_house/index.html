Floyd, Virginia

More than a stay — a place to belong for a while

Floyd is a small Blue Ridge Mountain town known for its live bluegrass and old-time music, working artist studios and a slower pace of life. Stay at the house, but spend your days in town.

Live Music at the Floyd Country Store

Just 0.7 miles from the house — an easy walk on continuous sidewalk — the historic Floyd Country Store hosts the Friday Night Jamboree: a beloved weekly tradition of live bluegrass, old-time music and flatfoot dancing that draws locals and travelers alike. Old-time jam sessions spill out onto the sidewalk most weekends, too.

Hiking & the Blue Ridge Parkway

The Blue Ridge Parkway is about 4 miles from the house, with overlooks and trailheads offering waterfalls, ridge-line hikes and long mountain views in every direction. Downtown Floyd is walkable from the house, but for hiking and river trips further out, a car is recommended.

  • Local Craft & Art

    Studios, galleries and makers throughout town — pottery, woodwork, textiles and more.

  • Farm-to-Table Dining

    Small-batch coffee, local produce and Appalachian-inspired kitchens downtown.

  • Trails for Every Level

    From easy overlook walks to full ridge-line day hikes, minutes from the door.
