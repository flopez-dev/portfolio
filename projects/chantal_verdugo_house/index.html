Floyd, Virginia

Bluegrass on Friday, the Parkway on Saturday

We live in a small Blue Ridge Mountain town known for its live bluegrass and old-time music, working artist studios and a slower pace of life. Stay at our house, but spend your days in town.

Live Music at the Floyd Country Store

The historic Floyd Country Store is just 0.7 miles from our house, an easy walk on continuous sidewalk. It hosts the Friday Night Jamboree, a beloved weekly tradition of live bluegrass, old-time music and flatfoot dancing that draws locals and travelers alike. Old-time jam sessions spill out onto the sidewalk most weekends, too.

Hiking & the Blue Ridge Parkway

The Blue Ridge Parkway is about 4 miles from our house, with overlooks and trailheads offering waterfalls, ridge-line hikes and long mountain views in every direction. Downtown Floyd is walkable from the house, but for hiking and river trips further out, we'd recommend a car.

  • Local Craft & Art

    Studios, galleries and makers throughout town, with pottery, woodwork, textiles and more.

  • Farm-to-Table Dining

    Small-batch coffee, local produce and Appalachian-inspired kitchens downtown.

  • Trails for Every Level

    From easy overlook walks to full ridge-line day hikes, minutes from the door.
