Floyd, Virginia

More than a stay, a place to belong for a while

Floyd is a small Blue Ridge Mountain town known for its live bluegrass and old-time music, working artist studios and a slower pace of life. Stay at the house, but spend your days in town.

Live Music at the Floyd Country Store

Just 0.7 miles from the house, an easy walk on continuous sidewalk, the historic Floyd Country Store at 206 S. Locust St. is part general store, part café and soda fountain, and part live-music hall. The Friday Night Jamboree runs 6:30 to 10pm, and the week also brings Sunday Music Jams and Honky Tonk Thursdays. Stop in for a Reuben or hand-dipped ice cream between sets. They're closed Mondays, open 11am to 7pm the rest of the week, later on Friday and Saturday.

Visit floydcountrystore.com (opens in a new tab)

Hiking & the Blue Ridge Parkway

The Blue Ridge Parkway is about 4 miles from the house, part of a 469-mile scenic drive through the Virginia and North Carolina mountains with no entrance fee. The stretch near Floyd has overlooks, waterfalls and ridge-line trailheads, and the full parkway runs south past landmarks like Linville Falls. Downtown Floyd is walkable from the house, but for hiking and river trips further out, a car is recommended.

Visit blueridgeparkway.org (opens in a new tab)

Mabry Mill

Further along the Parkway, at milepost 176, is Mabry Mill — one of the most-photographed spots on the entire 469-mile drive. Ed and Lizzy Mabry built the still-turning water-powered gristmill in the early 1900s, set on its own pond with short walking trails around the grounds. It's especially striking in fall, when the trees along the water turn red and gold.

Visit nps.gov/blri (opens in a new tab)

FAQ

Questions guests ask us

Is there parking at the house?

Yes. We have a free residential garage with one space. Plus the driveway allows 4 cars to be comfortably parked.

Do we need to meet you to check in?

No. We use a self check-in smartlock, so you can arrive and get settled without waiting on us.

Are there stairs?

Practically, you will be on one level. The master bedroom, bedroom 1, bedroom 2, both bathrooms, kitchen, dining room and living room are all on the same level and there is just one step to get to the guest bedroom, washer/dryer and garage.

How far is the house from the Floyd Country Store?

About 0.7 miles, an easy walk on continuous sidewalk to the Friday Night Jamboree.

Is the whole house included in our stay?

Yes, except for the basement and a separate outbuilding. Those are our own private space and stay locked.
