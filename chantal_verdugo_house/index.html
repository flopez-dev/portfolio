About the House

A handcrafted mountain hideaway

Tucked into a quiet hillside just outside downtown Floyd, The Chantal Verdugo House pairs timber-frame warmth with modern comfort. Reclaimed wood beams, a wood-burning stone fireplace and wide porch views were built for slow mornings with coffee and long evenings under the stars.

The house comfortably sleeps up to eight guests across three bedrooms, with an open-concept living and kitchen area that spills out onto a covered deck. Whether you're here for a bluegrass weekend or a week unplugged in the Blue Ridge, the house is set up to make you feel at home.

Floyd, Virginia

More than a stay — a place to belong for a while

Floyd is a small Blue Ridge Mountain town known for its live bluegrass and old-time music, working artist studios and a slower pace of life. Stay at the house, but spend your days in town.

Live Music at the Floyd Country Store

Just ten minutes from the house, the historic Floyd Country Store hosts the Friday Night Jamboree — a beloved weekly tradition of live bluegrass, old-time music and flatfoot dancing that draws locals and travelers alike. Old-time jam sessions spill out onto the sidewalk most weekends, too.

Hiking & the Blue Ridge Parkway

The house sits a short drive from several Blue Ridge Parkway overlooks and trailheads, with waterfalls, ridge-line hikes and long mountain views in every direction. Pack a picnic, chase a sunset, or just pull off at an overlook on the way into town.

  • Local Craft & Art

    Studios, galleries and makers throughout town — pottery, woodwork, textiles and more.

  • Farm-to-Table Dining

    Small-batch coffee, local produce and Appalachian-inspired kitchens downtown.

  • Trails for Every Level

    From easy overlook walks to full ridge-line day hikes, minutes from the door.
