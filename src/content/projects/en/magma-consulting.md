Magma has spent twenty years launching mobile virtual network operators and fibre
networks. Their site ran on WordPress, with the maintenance and updates that drags
along, for a company whose business does not depend on publishing something every week.

The new version reorganises the content they already had around what a carrier looks for
on arrival: what they know how to do, who they've done it with, and how to start. The
legal notice, privacy policy and cookie policy moved to pages of their own, with clean
URLs.

**Still in progress.** The structure and the legal pages are done; the final copy and the
team photographs are not, and the domain does not point here yet.
