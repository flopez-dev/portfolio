INMICA installs, repairs and supplies parts for the bottling and packaging lines of the
wineries around La Mancha. Their customer is a maintenance manager with a stopped
machine, and that customer is not shopping around: they are looking for someone to call.

The page is built around that call. What they do and where they work comes first, and
the phone number and the orders WhatsApp stay visible on every screen size rather than
hiding behind a form. Below it sit the three things a maintenance manager checks before
picking up: which machines they handle, that they run their own workshop and stock, and
who is behind the company.

One page, no content manager, nothing in it that expires. It opens just as fast on a
phone, inside a plant, on two bars of signal.
