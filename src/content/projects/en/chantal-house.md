A vacation rental lives inside a booking platform: the platform writes the listing,
orders the photos and sets the commission. A site of your own doesn't replace that, but
it gives you something the platform never will — somewhere to send the people who
already know you.

The site tells the house the way a host would. All on one level, sleeps eight, walking
distance from the Country Store and four miles from the Blue Ridge Parkway. Then the
things a guest always asks before booking: how you get in, what's in the kitchen, where
you park, what there is to do nearby.

It runs on its own domain and deploys itself. Keeping it online costs what the domain
costs.
